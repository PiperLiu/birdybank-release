## 中文

- 新增「清理丢失记录」：在文件夹管理中选择来源，先检查并预览，再确认移除已删除原片的图库记录。保留原有标注和历史，可通过恢复入口找回；离线或无权限的来源会被跳过。
- 新增 BirdyLink 局域网原片互传，配对后可收发静态 JPEG、PNG、HEIC 原片，收到的照片由用户选择加入图库；修复 Windows 收件保存失败的问题。
- 完善同一电脑、同一系统下的图库迁移，支持明确确认后替换目标图库，并保留可恢复的本地备份。迁移包不替代原始照片、视频和音频的备份。
- 修复图库迁移后部分视频预览无法正常恢复的问题，改进清理过程的进度与停止操作。

## English

- Adds **Clean Missing** in Folder Manager: select sources, review a fresh preview, then confirm removal of records whose originals were deleted. Annotations and history remain recoverable; offline or inaccessible sources are skipped.
- Adds BirdyLink LAN transfer for original static JPEG, PNG and HEIC photos after pairing. Received photos enter the library only when you choose to import them. Fixes incoming-photo storage failures on Windows.
- Improves library migration between installations on the same computer and operating system, including explicitly confirmed replacement and a restorable local backup. Migration packages do not replace backups of original photos, videos or audio.
- Fixes recovery of some video previews after library migration and improves cleanup progress and cancellation.
